### Aniqlangan faktlar

- Approval immutable args+fingerprint bilan bog'langan; descriptive key (persona) o'zgarsa approval bekor bo'lmaydi.
- `agent_loop.py`: persisted, budget-cheklangan (max_steps/max_calls/deadline), savepoint rollback, repeated-action blok, lease expire = escalate (avtomatik retry yo'q).

- `business_graph.py`: cross-system read-model, conflict hech qachon jimgina hal qilinmaydi, authority source I/O dan oldin tekshiriladi.

### Asosiy xavflar / kamchiliklar
1. **Live acceptance yo'q** — barcha testlar fake provider/offline. Google/CRM/Telegram/Sheets real servis bilan sinovdan o'tmagan.
2. **Runner POSIX-only** — Windows runner yo'q, `portable_fs` Windowsda yiqiladi (loyihada bu "ataylab" deb yozilgan).
3. **Dual architecture** — eski `app/` (approvals, ladder, customer360, storage SQLite) va yangi `platform_runtime/` + `platform_api.py` parallel yashayapti. Legacy mutation'lar 410 bilan o'chirilgan, lekin kod bazada qolgan.

5. **Testlar mock'ka bog'langan** — engine yaxshi testlangan, lekin haqiqiy xatolar (provider drift, auth expiry, network) sinovdan o'tmagan.
6. **Dependency isolation yo'q** — `runtime_tests` (dependency-free deb ataladi) aslida `cryptography`ni talab qiladi.

- **Yozish yo'li YO'Q.** Modelni tahrirlay oladigan tizim har bir hodisa tayanadigan
  **identitetning muallifi** bo'lardi. Platforma — OT **o'quvchisi**.
- **Segment, satr prefiksi emas.** `'zavod-10'.startswith('zavod-1')` = `True` —

### Ushlandan nuqsonlar — 3 ta HAQIQIY modul nuqsoni
1. `children`/`descendants` intern tugunni **umuman qabul qilmasdi** (bitta qat'iy

2. `children` **noto'g'ri `level`** qaytarardi: `declared[len(parent)-1]` —
   ota-onaning darajasi, bir pog'ona sayoz. `level` + yangi `child_level` ajratildi.
3. **Graf preflight Sheets uchun `allowed_connections` ni umuman tekshirmasdi.**
   Shart faqat `connectors.read`/`database.read` uchun edi, lekin `sheets.rows`
   ulanishni **reestrdan** oladi. Ya'ni **P1 dan beri**: `allowed_connections:['erp']`

   qo'shildi (args yoki reestr — ikkala yo'l). Nega ilgari ushlanmadi: grafikning
   o'z fixture'ida Sheets manbasi doim **ikkinchi** edi va `erp` birinchi javob
   berardi. Shu sabab **2 regressiya testi** yozildi.
